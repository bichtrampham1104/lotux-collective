# Health Access Vietnam

**Status:** Planned  
**Purpose:** Public-interest information design and health-service navigation.

## Question
What information barriers may make it harder for people in Vietnam, including informal workers, to navigate preventive care and health services?

## Possible methods
- Review dated, official public information and record source links.
- Map a selected information journey.
- Identify confusing steps, missing explanations and accessibility barriers.
- Draft plain-language Vietnamese guides and test readability.
- Seek appropriate consent and ethics guidance before conducting or analysing interviews.

## Possible outputs
Source inventory, journey map, information-gap summary, practical guide and limitations note.

## Safeguards
Do not collect health IDs or sensitive personal information. Clearly separate official requirements from interpretation. Do not imply eligibility or replace advice from official agencies or healthcare professionals.
