A little about us

Health technology should work for people.

LOTUX Collective is an independent learning and project space based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. We bring together technology, public health, design and communication to ask a simple question: how can digital systems serve people more fairly?

We are especially interested in the everyday realities behind health data — who is represented, who is missing, who can access care, and who gets to make decisions.

Independent work in progress. Not a healthcare provider, clinical service or formal research institution.
